About me

I'm Sidharth Dutta, a computer engineering major at Georgia Tech. I'm from Cary, North Carolina, and I started in August 2026. My interests are embedded systems, chip design, and PCB design.

In high school I was president of the Adopt-a-Grandparent club. We exchanged more than 400 letters with older adults and organized five events. I also worked at the Brier Pointe retirement center for over two years.

From June 2024 to August 2026 I taught at American Robotics Academy in Cary. I designed week-long lessons on robotics, basic circuits, and Arduino for middle school students and ran a battle bot challenge.

In summer 2025 I did research at UNC Wilmington and the NC School of Science and Math on a computational design for a Huntington's disease treatment. I worked on codon optimization and plasmid design, presented at a university symposium, and co-authored a research paper.

At Georgia Tech I am on the electrical team at HyTech Racing and a member of the GT iOS Club and Silicon Jackets. At HackGT 13 my team built Window, a pen-pal app, and I did the screens and design.

Outside class I do calligraphy, photography, cycling, and calisthenics. See the hobbies page.
