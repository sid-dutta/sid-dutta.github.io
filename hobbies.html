Hobbies

Calligraphy

I practice calligraphy with fountain pens. The journal page in the photo is flowers and vines drawn in dark red ink.

My practice sheets record the pen angle, 30 degrees on one of them, next to the stroke drills. I also ran a pen-pal club in high school, which is where my interest in handwriting and letters comes from.
