Discovery Project: lidar belt for blind users

The full Discovery Project feature will be added after the showcase. PCB design at HyTech Racing

This project was part of electrical onboarding at HyTech Racing, Georgia Tech's Formula SAE electric race team. I designed a keychain PCB powered over USB-C. A 555 timer flashes an amber LED at a 53% duty cycle, set by the resistor and capacitor values.

I laid out the board in Altium Designer (first photo) and soldered components by hand (second photo).

Research: protein design for Huntington's disease

Summer 2025, UNC Wilmington and the NC School of Science and Math. We designed a protein as a possible Huntington's disease therapy, using regions of 2GSG that bind polyglutamine and a humanized 3EBA scaffold. The protein has four parts: an anti-transferrin receptor arm that delivers it into the brain, an intrabody that binds mutant huntingtin, a peptide linker, and a LAMP1 component that sends the captured protein to the lysosome.

I worked on codon optimization and plasmid design. The anti-TfR sequences went into a pET28 backbone for E. coli, and the other sequences went into a pIVTRup backbone for human cells. I also supported plasmid production and purification, presented the work at a university symposium, and co-authored a research paper.

Window: a pen-pal app

Window was my team's project at HackGT 13 in September 2026. Users pick a city they want to visit and are matched with someone who lives there. Each day they exchange a photo and a short voice note. AI translates the captions and voice notes and adds a note about local culture. A passport collects stamps from the places they have shared.

Hardware teammates built Raspberry Pi devices that show the partner's local sky and send a knock. I did the app's screens and visual design.

Team
Four people: two on software, two on hardware
Built with
Expo, React Native, Supabase, FastAPI, Raspberry Pi
